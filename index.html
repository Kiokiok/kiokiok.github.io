About me

I've always had a keen interest in video games and the work that goes into them for as long as I can remember. Today this curiosity is stronger than ever and every day drives my passion for my work. I'm constantly pushing myself and trying new things to improve my skills and knowledge in order to try and bring great experiences to people.

I strongly believe making video games is a team effort, and I hope to be able to bring as much to my peers as they could bring to me. Throughout the years, I have acquired various skills on many tools, ranging from programming language, to engines, to 3D and 2D software. I'm very passionate about my work in games, and I enjoy working in many different fields of the trade, albeit programming remains my favorite domain to explore.

My favorite game genres include Action RPGs, Strategy RPGs, Sandbox survivals and Tactics games. During my free time, I enjoy learning about new things, reading mangas and fantasy novels, playing D&D, or trying out my hand at art.

A procedural multiplayer survival adventure game.

I worked full time on this project for all of 2023, with the end goal of co-founding a studio. I left the team at the beginning of 2024 for personal reasons, but the project was a lot of fun to work on and I learned a lot while working on it. I was the sole programmer on the team, and handled pretty much every technical aspect of it.

The game features an open, vertical, procedural world explorable in multiplayer. The players have many tools available to help them traverse the atypical world. The goal of the game is to go down further and further down in an unexplored abyss and uncover its mysteries and history.

At the time of me leaving, most of the underlying engine was functional (world streaming, networking) and gameplay mechanics were starting to get implemented. The game uses a data-driven approach to a lot of things: Entity declaration, Items declaration, Character controller configuration, among other things. This was done to help development time and later on help bring modding support to the game.

Technical information

  • Engine: Godot
  • Responsible for the entirety of the programming
  • Languages used: C#, LUA, C++, GDScript

Notes

  • Integrated Flecs on top of Godot engine (ECS)
  • Built a network entity replication system on top of Enet (creation, deletion, state change)
  • Built a custom renderer system to render entities efficiently
  • Used a data-driven approach to declare entities, items, crafting recipes, using the LUA scripting language
  • Built a basic network interest manager and world streaming system
  • Built a custom entity spawning system on top of VoxelTools
  • Built an extensive state machine system for the character controller
  • Implemented the character controller and various gameplay tools
  • UI programming
